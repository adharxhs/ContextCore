Benchmark snapshot

50 fixed cases, real engine, base 145a25c. Token reduction ~59%; mean evidence recall 80–83%. Sixteen over-budget cases are protected-linked and engine-flagged. Known open gaps: QA-dependency and long-history turns, the unprotected oversized sentence, and protected-span precision. No same-model answer comparison has run, so no quality claim is made.
